## 1. ENGLISH
### The Universal Reality: A Challenge Shared Across the Globe
On coasts across the globe, financial speculation and real estate pressure are disconnecting families from their ancestral lands. Corsica has become a key laboratory for this universal challenge.
### Our Response: The FSUCIETÀ Initiative
We chose science, open data, and rigorous transparency. We compiled 50 Scientific & Legal Analytical Axes combining European satellite imagery (Copernicus / Sentinel-2), public land records (DVF), and corporate registries (INPI).
### The International Call
We are making this neutral dataset available to global civil society, journalists (ICIJ, Bellingcat), and researchers to audit, verify, and replicate this open methodology.
**Facts are neutral. Data is public. Information is a public good.**
